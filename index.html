// 02 LIVE · 2026 AI · RAG

Internal HR Assistant — RAG over a Private Corpus

SIB · Kénitra

A retrieval-augmented assistant answering employee questions over a private HR corpus — permission filtering applied before similarity is ever computed, and explicit refusal when the corpus cannot support an answer.

PROBLEM

HR answered the same policy questions every week while the answers sat in documents nobody could search. Any assistant over that corpus risks the opposite failure: surfacing a salary or a contract clause to the wrong employee.

APPROACH

Hybrid retrieval — BAAI/bge-m3 embeddings with a tuned relevance floor, plus a lexical rescue path for exact HR terms that vectors miss. Access rights are a SQL predicate applied before scoring, so a forbidden chunk is never ranked. Embeddings run in a dedicated sidecar because the 2–3 GB model would not fit the production VPS.

OUTCOME

Answers stay grounded in the corpus, and questions it cannot cover are logged into a weekly gap digest that drives what gets documented next. Rolled out to a pilot group before general access.
